## Overview

### Adoption Vision

**By 2056, I will be:**
- The primary programming language in Rwanda
- A leading language in Africa
- A significant language globally
- The language of choice for culturally-conscious developers

### Adoption Principles

1. **Cultural First**: Lead with cultural identity
2. **Local to Global**: Start in Rwanda, expand to Africa, then world
3. **Quality Over Quantity**: Build quality ecosystem first
4. **Community Driven**: Let community drive adoption
5. **Patience**: Adoption takes decades, not years
