## Governance Philosophy

The I Programming Language is governed by the following principles:

- **Community-Driven**: Decisions are made with community input
- **Merit-Based**: Leadership is based on contribution and expertise
- **Transparent**: All processes are open and documented
- **Inclusive**: Diverse perspectives are valued and sought
- **Long-Term Focus**: Decisions consider the 30-year horizon
- **African-Centric**: The project serves African developers first

## Decision Making

### Decision Types

#### Strategic Decisions
- Made by Board of Directors
- Require board approval
- Documented in board minutes

#### Technical Decisions
- Made by Technical Steering Committee
- Require consensus or majority vote
- Documented in TSC meeting notes

#### Community Decisions
- Made by Community Committee
- Require community input
- Documented in community forums

#### Operational Decisions
- Made by working group leads
- Require working group consensus
- Documented in working group meetings

### Decision Process

1. **Proposal**: Submit a proposal with rationale
2. **Discussion**: Open discussion period (minimum 2 weeks)
3. **Review**: Committee reviews and deliberates
4. **Decision**: Vote or consensus decision
5. **Communication**: Decision communicated to stakeholders
6. **Documentation**: Decision documented and archived

### Voting

- **Consensus**: Preferred method for all decisions
- **Majority Vote**: Used when consensus cannot be reached
- **Supermajority**: Required for major changes (2/3 vote)
- **Unanimous**: Required for constitutional changes

### Appeals

Decisions can be appealed to:
- TSC decisions → Board of Directors
- Working group decisions → TSC
- Community committee decisions → Board of Directors

## Election Process

### Eligibility

- Must be a contributor for at least 6 months
- Must have made significant contributions
- Must be in good standing with code of conduct
- Must be nominated by two existing members

### Nomination Process

1. **Nomination**: Submit nomination with rationale
2. **Seconding**: Requires two seconds from existing members
3. **Acceptance**: Nominee must accept nomination
4. **Campaign**: Nominee presents vision and qualifications
5. **Question Period**: Community asks questions
6. **Voting**: Community votes
7. **Announcement**: Results announced

### Voting

- **Eligible Voters**: All contributors with 6+ months of activity
- **Voting Period**: 2 weeks
- **Voting Method**: Secure online voting
- **Quorum**: Minimum 25% of eligible voters
- **Majority**: Simple majority required

### Terms

- Board of Directors: 2 years
- TSC: 1 year
- Community Committee: 1 year
- Security Committee: 1 year

### Term Limits

- Board of Directors: Maximum 3 consecutive terms
- TSC: No term limit
- Other committees: Maximum 2 consecutive terms

## Conflict Resolution

### Types of Conflicts

- **Technical Disagreements**: Technical decisions
- **Personal Conflicts**: Interpersonal issues
- **Process Disagreements**: Governance process issues
- **Code of Conduct Violations**: Conduct issues

### Resolution Process

#### Step 1: Direct Resolution
- Parties attempt direct resolution
- Mediator can be requested
- Document resolution attempt

#### Step 2: Committee Involvement
- Relevant committee reviews the conflict
- Committee provides mediation
- Committee makes recommendation

#### Step 3: Escalation
- If unresolved, escalate to Board of Directors
- Board makes final decision
- Decision is binding

### Code of Conduct Violations

See [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) for detailed process.

## Amendments

### Process

1. **Proposal**: Submit amendment proposal
2. **Discussion**: Community discussion (4 weeks)
3. **Review**: Committee review and recommendation
4. **Vote**: Community vote
5. **Approval**: Unanimous board approval
6. **Implementation**: Amendment implemented

### Requirements

- Supermajority community vote (2/3)
- Unanimous board approval
- Documented rationale
- Implementation plan
